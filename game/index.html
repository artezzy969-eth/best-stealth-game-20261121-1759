SHADOW COURIER

A top-down stealth game

Night shift at the old Meridian depot. You are the courier nobody is supposed to see. Cross each floor, collect the parcels, and slip out the green exit — while the guards sweep their lamps across the floor. Crouch in the shadows, freeze behind the crates, and never let a vision cone touch you.

  • WASD / ↑←↓→ — move
  • Shift (hold) — crouch: slower, quieter, harder to spot
  • P or Esc — pause
  • M — mute sound  ·  R — restart level
  • Mouse — click the buttons on every screen

Guards patrol fixed routes and watch a cone of light ahead of them. Inside a cone you are spotted faster when you stand, run, or make noise; crouching in shadow or behind crates keeps you hidden. A guard who grows suspicious walks over to check — break line of sight and stay quiet until the alert fades. Collect every parcel on the floor, then reach the green exit. Being caught ends the run. Three floors, one quiet courier.
